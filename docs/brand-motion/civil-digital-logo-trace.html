Civil Digital logo trace

The Civil Digital knot and logotype drawn with anime.js svg.createDrawable(), in the same blueprint style as the Dispatch version.

The idea

The knot is drawn the way you would draw it by hand: one continuous figure-of-eight. When the real mark fills in and the pen's ribbon lifts away, the gaps show the strand passing under, so the weave appears as the last step.

Built on the mark's own geometry

  • The guides are the knot's 45° diagonals through its centre, plus its top, centre and base lines.
  • The logotype's caps are scaled to the knot's height, so one cap line and one baseline run through the whole lockup.
  • The lavender dots on the i and the A land last, echoing the Dispatch dot.
